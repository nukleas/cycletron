Welcome

Cycletron

AI-first strudel music engine. A quick tour, then you're free.

  • ⌘⇧P command palette — every action, every file
  • ⌘O / ⌘S open / save
  • ⌘↩ play · Esc stop
  • ⌘F find & replace

The AI assistant is optional

Cycletron runs fully by hand — editor, samples, MIDI, the whole engine. The AI composer is there if you want it, and it stays off until you turn it on.

When you're ready, hit Enable AI… in the AI panel and pick a provider. It's your call, any time.

Library location

Where Cycletron keeps the patterns you save and the MIDI conversions you import. You can move it later from Preferences.

—

You're all set

Press Play, open Examples, and load Lesson 1. The left panel is the AI Operator; Files is your library.

Help → User Guide, Keyboard Shortcuts, and Dialect cover the rest. If anything breaks, Help → Show Logs for a diagnostic dump.

Turn on the AI assistant?

Cycletron includes an AI assistant that can write, edit, and arrange patterns with you. It's yours to switch on — or leave off.

Turning it on sends your prompts and the code in your editor to your chosen model provider. Your call.

You can run Cycletron fully by hand. You'll just be leaving some of its range on the table.

                Samples
                
            

            

                
Sample set

                
The active set is the base sound world for playback and export — switching reloads the audio engine. The strudel-rs set (Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm CC-BY-3.0, uzu drumkit, uzu wavetables, Dirt-Samples, ~300 MB) is fetched from the upstream repos; define more sets in sample-sets.json in the app data folder.

                

                
                
Packs

                
Optional sample packs live in your library under Packs/. Enabled packs add their banks on top of the active set.

Ableton Link

                    
                    

                    
Shares tempo and bar phase with Ableton Live, Bitwig and any other Link app on the network. Discovery is automatic — there is nothing to configure. While Link is on the session owns the tempo, so a pattern's setbpm is ignored, and Play waits for the shared bar line before starting.

                

                

                    
OSC Output

                    
                    
                    
                    

                    
Streams /cycletron/cycle, /cycletron/transport and /cycletron/hap — point Hydra, Resolume, TouchDesigner or a lighting rig at it. Onsets are frame-accurate (~16 ms), which suits visuals rather than driving a sampler.
